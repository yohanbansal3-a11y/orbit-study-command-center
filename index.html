Google Calendar · read-only

Not connected

Orbit reads your Google Calendar and updates its own schedule. It never creates, edits, or deletes events in Google Calendar. Calendar dates and times take priority over Orbit timetable entries.

Not connected. Add your OAuth Client ID and connect once. When you reopen Orbit, it will attempt a silent refresh and sync automatically if Google allows it.

Only the Google Calendar read-only permission is requested. The access token stays in memory and is never stored in Supabase. If silent authorization is unavailable or the token expires, click Connect Google Calendar.
